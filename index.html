CASE STUDY 01 / 03 · NETWORK PLANNING

Network Lab | Airline Route Screening

I screened 200 hypothetical airline markets and tested 463 historical replay decisions against a demand-only baseline. The additional model complexity showed no detectable incremental ranking improvement at the tested resolution. The result is a reproducible decision-screening study, not a claim of accurate route prediction.

Public-data ingestion and reconciliation
Carrier and market competition reconstruction
Fixed-gauge, predefined schedule screening
Documented data-grain and mapping corrections
Historical replay against a demand baseline
Clear limits on predictive resolution
