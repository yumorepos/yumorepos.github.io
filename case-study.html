What I was trying to solve

Funding-rate strategies can look profitable in historical data. I wanted to know whether the apparent opportunity survived real trading costs, execution uncertainty, concentrated samples, and different market conditions — and whether the surrounding software could fail safely.

The project evolved into a research and execution system with exchange integrations, backtests, simulated and limited real trading, ledgers, alerts, automated tests, and documented risk controls.

How I approached the problem

01 / BUILDImplement the workflowData ingestion, signal evaluation, simulation, and controlled execution components.
02 / CHALLENGEAudit the evidenceCheck concentration, leverage, cost assumptions, trade records, and failure cases.
03 / DECIDERespect the resultNarrow unsupported claims, disarm trading, and preserve the research.

What the audits found

The headline backtest was not generalizable. Twenty of its first twenty-three trades came from one asset. A later test also showed that leveraged live-exit rules and the original unleveraged backtest exit assumptions were not equivalent.

Software controls needed operational validation. Real execution uncovered issues including incomplete close handling, a misrecorded losing trade, and lost alerts. Those findings informed code and observability changes.

A separate carry strategy was narrower than it first appeared. Historical spot/perpetual funding-carry research suggested positive funding income, but the funding was decaying, liquidity limited capacity, and its Sharpe statistic did not withstand multiple-testing adjustment. The later carry executor remained dry-run and did not implement live order placement.

The result I am comfortable claiming

I demonstrated system design, testing, auditability, and a willingness to retire an idea when its practical economics did not work. I did not establish a repeatable or scalable profitable trading strategy. On June 29, 2026, the automated trading services were wound down and the records were preserved.

Important evidence limits

The underlying source repository is private and the original historical audit records are not independently reproducible from this public page. The project is no longer an active trading service. Historical performance estimates are not forecasts or investment recommendations.
